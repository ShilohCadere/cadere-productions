What matters changes the answer.

Weigh explores a guided path from a question to a comparison matrix. Define the options, name the criteria, and decide their importance. Then explore how a different set of priorities changes the picture, without losing the original comparison.

A decision you can explain.

A total can be useful, but the reasoning matters more. The planned experience keeps the options, criteria, and importance weights visible, so a changing result is something to understand rather than simply accept.

The design direction

  • Guided entry alongside a flexible matrix editor.
  • Importance weighting based on your own priorities.
  • Temporary scenario exploration before committing a change.

Where the project stands

A product concept in development. The matrix shown on this page contains illustrative example scores; it is not a functioning Weigh application.
