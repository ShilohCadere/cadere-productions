Inside the current build

One journey.
Room for alternatives.

Furlong’s current Trip Workspace brings accommodation comparisons, flights, excursions, ground transport, and budget details into the same journey. Destination navigation follows the route, with city pages for a closer view of local plans.

The development is being shaped by a Europe trip. Its S1 and L1 routes keep different trip versions available for comparison, while saved quotes, dates, links, and notes can be edited as the research improves.

Implemented in the development build

  • Username-based accounts with trips saved to their owner.
  • Trip creation, overview, dated itinerary days, and editable activities.
  • Saved accommodation options with editable quotes, dates, booking links, and notes.
  • Route scenarios with their own selections and budget settings.
  • City pages connecting accommodation, local ideas, and itinerary days.
  • A Budget Playground for temporary comparisons, plus a separate current-plan expense ledger.
02 / Budget Playground

Try a possibility.
See what it changes.

Swap a stay, compare a castle night, or add a hypothetical cost or saving. The Playground recalculates the estimate while keeping saved selections and recorded expenses intact.

Unpriced choices are flagged, so a missing quote stays visible in the comparison. Resetting the experiment returns the view to its saved baseline.

03 / City notebook

A closer look
at each destination.

Each city page brings together the selected stay, available accommodation options, local ideas, and dated itinerary entries for the chosen route.

Edinburgh shows a separate city stay and castle night. Each keeps its own dates and cost, making their place in the trip easier to follow.
