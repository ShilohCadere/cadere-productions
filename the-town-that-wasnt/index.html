A town that outgrew its fiction.

It began as a trap town: a fictional place added to a map. Then a real settlement grew around the name. Later changes absorbed it into another identity, leaving evidence scattered across maps, records, and the places people still live.

Follow evidence at your own pace.

The approved concept centers on nonlinear exploration: move through an illustrated map, examine scenes, and connect discoveries in an automatic journal and deduction board. Present residents want the original name restored, but the question of what makes a town itself has more than one answer.

The concept direction

  • An illustrated map linking explorable scenes and evidence.
  • An automatic journal and a deduction board for connections.
  • Optional graduated hints, with room to investigate freely.

Where the project stands

Working story canon and interaction direction have been developed. The project remains in design, without a released game. The map on this page is an editorial illustration, not a playable location.
