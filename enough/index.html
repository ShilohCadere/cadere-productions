Curiosity with a finish line.

Where Weigh helps compare options, Enough helps investigate a question: what is known, what supports it, what is still uncertain, and what actually needs another look.

Two ways in. One place to think.

The design allows a question or existing research to become the starting point. A user-defined finish line sits alongside evidence-based signals, helping distinguish a useful next step from another lap through the same material.

The concept direction

  • A flexible workspace for questions, evidence, sources, and unknowns.
  • Two entry points: begin with a question or bring research already gathered.
  • A personal finish line informed by the evidence collected.

Where the project stands

A developed research-companion concept, currently on the drawing board. Enough is the project name. This page presents the direction; an interactive product has not been released.
