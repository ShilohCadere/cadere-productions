The everyday becomes an exhibit.

Historically grounded rooms give familiar things the distance they need to become strange again. Object cabinets, evidence, and reconstructed workplaces let visitors wander at their own pace. The unseen Curator and Archivist leave traces of their own workplace in the margins.

An ordinary thing, seen differently.

A future perspective gives familiar objects and working lives a new context. Visitors can follow an interest across carefully framed exhibits, while small traces of the unseen Curator and Archivist suggest another story behind the labels.

The design direction

  • Galleries of objects, ideas, and occupations from the mid-2020s and earlier.
  • Room formats designed around the subject, from specimen cabinets to reconstructed workplaces.
  • Open exploration, without scores or progress-locked exhibits.

Where the project stands

Museum design and collection development are in progress. The featured floppy disk is an illustrative exhibit treatment, not a released collection entry.
