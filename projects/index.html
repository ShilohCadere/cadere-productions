Flagships + experiments

Room to go deep.
Room to try something.

Furlong and Creature Cabinet are the studio’s flagship projects: substantial work with distinct identities and room to grow.

Smaller experiments give a focused idea space to be tested and finished. Only selected work earns a place in this collection; the full notebook stays on the studio desk.

Weigh, Enough, The Obsolescence Museum, The Town That Wasn’t, and Observatory of Everything extend that collection. Their design stages are shown above; a place in the portfolio is not a release-date commitment.

Applications

Tools for the real world.

Furlong begins with travel planning. Weigh explores how to make the reasoning behind a decision visible. Enough brings research questions and supporting evidence into a more focused workspace.

Experiences

Worlds worth a closer look.

Creature Cabinet follows an imagined ecology. The Obsolescence Museum makes the familiar strange again. The Town That Wasn’t follows a mystery through maps and memory. Observatory of Everything explores the possibilities of scale.
