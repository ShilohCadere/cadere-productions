A museum without a fixed size.

The original concept paired rotating Big and Small exhibits with a layered experience for different levels of curiosity. A digital version opens another possibility: move between scales, manipulate a view, or follow a connection that would be difficult to stage in a physical room.

A collection that can keep opening up.

The digital direction can grow around a permanent collection with changing featured exhibits. Earlier exhibits could remain explorable as new ones arrive. Featured exhibits could change as new material is ready, giving each addition time to develop.

The concept direction

  • Big and Small as complementary ways into the collection.
  • Potential for visitor-controlled scale and interactive exploration.
  • An After Dark direction, including a glowing, blacklight-inspired Small exhibit.
  • A possible permanent collection with rotating featured exhibits.

Where the project stands

Digital concept development, expanding on the earlier physical-attraction exercise. The interactions, collection structure, and exhibit cadence are being explored. The museum is not yet available to visit.
