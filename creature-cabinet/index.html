Look closer. Then look again.

The collection begins with the Glasshorn, Vitricervus lucentis, whose translucent antlers are only the start of its story. Specimen plates, curator notes, and relationships between species invite a slower kind of exploration, with the occasional institutional detail that raises another question.

An ecology, not just a collection.

The Glasshorn belongs to an interconnected world. Species relationships, field observations, and the quiet voice of the institution give each entry context. Discovery comes from attention, with a little dry humor in the margins.

The design direction

  • Vintage-inspired specimen plates and natural-history entries.
  • A cabinet to browse, with grid and list alternatives planned.
  • An interconnected ecology, from Glasshorn to Shiverleaf, Bellwren, and Ashmantle.

Where the project stands

Design and worldbuilding in progress. The creature illustration on this page is a stylized editorial interpretation. An interactive archive has not been released.
