About the studio

Small studio.
Big curiosity.

Cadere Productions is an independent studio making useful software and unusual interactive experiences. The work starts with curiosity and takes shape through careful craft.

A balanced independent studio

A practical streak.
An imaginative life.

Some projects begin with an everyday frustration: a trip scattered across tabs, or a choice that is difficult to compare. Others begin with a question: what might a natural-history archive from an unfamiliar world contain?

The studio makes room for both. Major projects alternate between practical tools and creative experiences, letting each kind of work inform the other.

“Useful software. Unusual experiences.”

How the work fits together

Individual identities.
A shared standard.

Furlong should feel like Furlong. Creature Cabinet should feel like a place of its own. Each project has its own visual language, purpose, and personality, with Cadere Productions as the studio behind it.

The connection is in the approach: clear interactions, considered details, accessible design, and enough restraint to let the subject lead.

Flagship projects receive sustained attention. Smaller experiments leave room for discovery. This site brings selected work together and makes its current stage clear.

Built to be sustainable

Finish thoughtfully.
Grow deliberately.

Success here means finished, polished work that can be shared and used. The studio values craftsmanship over volume and a sustainable pace over constant expansion.

Some projects may become commercial products when that supports their continued development. Others may remain small, independent experiences. Each project gets the model that suits its purpose.
